## Why not do it yourself?

A home-made AI agent looks cheaper until it has to work every day.

### Built by someone who has led software teams

I have spent years developing, designing and architecting software systems, and I have managed teams of developers building enterprise software with clean designs. I know what bad code and bad design look like, so I don't build them into your business. What I hand over is clear, tested and easy to change later.

### Guardrails, security and an audit trail

Home-made AI tools rarely include guardrails, security or an audit trail, and that is how customer and company data leaks. We build those in, so the system keeps working with your real systems, policies and staff long after go-live.

### We don't use AI for the sake of it

If the work can be automated without an AI agent, we do it that way. When AI does earn a place, it runs inside your own Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, works from your own documents, and a person reviews its output until you trust it.
