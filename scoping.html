// After Scoping

What shapes the quote

Price follows how deep the integration runs — connectors, workplace front door, native tools, compliance, and how much custom work is required. If we only connect a couple of tools, the build stays small. If we have to work deep inside your Microsoft/Google systems — or meet strict compliance rules — it takes longer.
